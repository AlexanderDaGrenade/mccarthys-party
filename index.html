Our story

Not a tour. A proper introduction.

The McCarthy family has been showing off Newfoundland and Labrador since 1982. You'll visit people in their homes, chat with fishermen on the wharf and sit down to tea with the local church group. You'll learn the language of the sea and maybe a song or two.

  • 01
    Guides from hereEvery guide grew up in the province. They know the culture, history and music firsthand, and they know the back roads where the best scenery is.
  • 02
    Smaller groups, better stopsWe cap coaches at 36–38 guests, so we fit at the small family restaurants and in the outports the big tours skip.
  • 03
    Sensible paceMost hotels are two-night stays and the days aren't rushed. You come home with stories, not jet lag from your own vacation.
  • 04
    Guaranteed departuresOnce a date is booked, the tour runs. You can plan flights with confidence.
